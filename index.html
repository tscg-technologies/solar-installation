Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Going Solar

Clear, straightforward answers to common questions about rooftop solar systems in India.

Solar photovoltaic (PV) panels installed on your rooftop capture sunlight and convert it into Direct Current (DC) electricity. A solar inverter then converts this DC power into Alternating Current (AC) electricity, which powers your home or business appliances. In an on-grid setup, any excess daytime electricity is exported to the utility grid via a bi-directional net meter, earning credits against your nighttime consumption.

As an illustrative benchmark in India, a standard on-grid rooftop solar system typically ranges between ₹55,000 and ₹65,000 per kW for residential systems (before any applicable government subsidies), while larger commercial and industrial systems benefit from economies of scale at roughly ₹48,000 to ₹55,000 per kW. Off-grid and hybrid systems cost more due to battery bank integration.

With modern high-efficiency monocrystalline solar modules (540 Wp+), approximately 85 to 100 sq. ft. of shadow-free rooftop area is required per 1 kW of solar capacity. For example, a 3 kW home solar system requires roughly 300 sq. ft., while a 5 kW system requires around 450–500 sq. ft.

An on-grid system works in sync with your local electricity utility grid without batteries, using net metering to bank excess power; it automatically shuts down during grid outages for line-worker safety (anti-islanding). An off-grid system operates independently of the grid and stores solar energy in batteries to run loads during power cuts or at night. A hybrid system combines both net metering and battery backup.

Physical on-site installation of mounting structures, panels, inverter, and wiring for a residential system (3 kW to 10 kW) typically takes 2 to 4 days. End-to-end commissioning—including site engineering, structure fabrication, and utility net-metering inspection—generally takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on local DISCOM timelines.

Solar PV systems require minimal maintenance. Rinsing the glass surface of the panels with clean water every 2 to 3 weeks (to remove dust, pollen, and bird droppings) prevents shading losses. Annual preventive checks of electrical connections, earthing pits, and inverter logs help ensure 25+ years of reliable performance.
